Transport fixture provenance

The two complete XID wire frames and their expected locations in xid.spec.js,
including the reused frame in mutation.spec.js,
come from xoolive/datalink, revision d3e3e5a5d2bda04fbee83832cb77e3050a87364c,
crates/acars/src/decode/xid.rs (MIT). The full original license is preserved in
test/upstream/datalink.license.txt and THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt.

Other transport byte encoders and fixtures are locally authored independent
boundary/checksum examples, distributed under this repository's MIT license.
CLNP encoders use RFC 994's checksum equations and wire format; ES-IS fixtures
use the common/ISH/redirect layout in RFC 995. Standards documents are references
and are not redistributed or presented as MIT-licensed source code.
