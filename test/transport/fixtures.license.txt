Transport fixture provenance

The two complete XID wire frames and their expected locations in xid.spec.js,
including the reused frame in mutation.spec.js,
come from xoolive/datalink, revision d3e3e5a5d2bda04fbee83832cb77e3050a87364c,
crates/acars/src/decode/xid.rs (MIT). The full original license is preserved in
test/upstream/datalink.license.txt and THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt.

Other transport byte encoders and fixtures are locally authored independent
boundary/checksum examples. The combined repository is GPL-3.0-or-later;
the original MIT notice is retained in docs/MIT-LICENSE.txt and applies to
previously MIT-distributed fixtures. New fixtures use the repository license.
CLNP encoders use RFC 994's checksum equations and wire format; ES-IS fixtures
use the common/ISH/redirect layout in RFC 995. Standards documents are references
and are not redistributed or presented as MIT-licensed source code.

esis-addresses.spec.js uses a locally authored count/list encoder informed by
NetBSD src revision 8c101a16ae09856a61284f770c2099b38186fe7c,
sys/netiso/esis.c (esis_eshinput/esis_shoutput) and esis.h (ISH timer constant).
The adapted implementation's BSD-3-Clause and IBM permission notices are
preserved in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt. No upstream fixture bodies were copied.
COTP service fixtures use RFC 905's ED/release rules and independent checksum
equations; those standards documents are not redistributed.

cotp-session/wire.js and its negotiation/state/edge-case tests independently
encode RFC 905 class-4 CR/CC/DT/ED/DR/DC/AK layouts and checksum equations.
Values are synthetic and no upstream code, tests or private captures were copied.

cotp-session/parameters.spec.js independently exercises RFC 905 section 13.2.3
duplicate-parameter last-occurrence semantics; every TLV stays in raw wire order.

cotp-session/preferred-size.spec.js independently exercises ITU-T X.224 (11/1995)
sections 6.5.4/6.5.5 k), 13.1 and 13.3.4 c), including synthetic unsigned
big-endian preferred sizes in 128-byte units, ordinary fallback and CC exclusivity.
No standard text, upstream code/tests or private capture bodies are redistributed.

cotp-session/timing.spec.js, large-input.spec.js, acknowledge.spec.js,
ordering.spec.js and release.spec.js use locally authored synthetic encoders
for X.224 sections 6.5/6.7/6.11, 12.2.3 and 13.3/13.9/13.10.
The independent expectations cover per-peer milliseconds, ROA negotiation,
local large-NSDU bounds, AK/FCC windows, EA matching, non-blocking ED dependencies
and passive DR/DC lifecycle evidence. No external code/tests were copied.

Compressed CLNP, X.233 options, IDRP/ATN security, XID parameters, HFDL
system-table and Aero tests use locally authored synthetic bytes and independent
checksum/wire encoders. Field-layout sources and revisions are pinned in
docs/upstreams.json, with original implementation notices preserved in
THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt. No private capture bodies or upstream tests were copied.
ATN checksum fixtures use closed-form modular equations; independent external
specification/capture verification remains outstanding.
